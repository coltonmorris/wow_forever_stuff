FineXP 1.1.0
============

A tiny XP-rate addon for WoW Classic.

Displays continuously-updating rolling rates for:
- last 30 seconds
- last 1 minute
- last 5 minutes
- last 15 minutes
- last 30 minutes
- last 45 minutes
- last 1 hour
- entire current addon session

Each row shows both XP/minute and XP/hour.

Commands:
  /fxp               show/hide
  /fxp reset         reset tracking
  /fxp lock          lock the frame
  /fxp unlock        unlock the frame; left-drag to move
  /fxp center        move it back to center
  /fxp scale 1.2     set scale from 0.5 to 2.5

Install:
1. Unzip FineXP.zip.
2. Put the FineXP folder inside your WoW Interface/AddOns folder.
3. Restart WoW or reload the UI.
4. FineXP should appear near the center of the screen.

Compatibility:
- TBC Classic Anniversary 2.5.6 (Interface 20506)
- Classic Era 1.15.9 (Interface 11509)
